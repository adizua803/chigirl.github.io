Classic Vanilla Cake

Soft, fluffy, and perfect for any celebration.

View Recipe
Ingredients:
  • 2½ cups flour
  • 1 cup butter
  • 1½ cups sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 2 tsp baking powder
  • 1 tbsp vanilla extract
Steps:
  1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C)
  2. Beat butter and sugar until fluffy
  3. Add eggs one at a time
  4. Add milk, vanilla, and dry ingredients
  5. Bake for 30–35 mins

New York Cheesecake

Creamy and rich with a graham cracker crust.

View Recipe
Ingredients:
  • 2 cups graham cracker crumbs
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 4 packs cream cheese
  • 1 cup sugar
  • 4 eggs
  • 1 cup sour cream
  • 1 tsp vanilla extract
Steps:
  1. Mix crumbs and butter, press into pan
  2. Beat cream cheese and sugar
  3. Add eggs one by one, then sour cream & vanilla
  4. Bake at 160°C for 50–60 mins
  5. Cool and chill 4+ hours

Soft Homemade Bread

Freshly baked and golden brown.

View Recipe
Ingredients:
  • 3½ cups flour
  • 1 cup warm water
  • 1 tbsp sugar
  • 2¼ tsp yeast
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 2 tbsp oil
Steps:
  1. Activate yeast in warm water + sugar
  2. Mix all ingredients and knead
  3. Let rise for 1 hour
  4. Shape and place in loaf pan
  5. Bake at 190°C for 25–30 mins
